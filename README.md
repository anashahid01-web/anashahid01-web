## 👋 About Me

I am a Lecturer in the [Department of Computer Science](https://numl.edu.pk/departments/178) at the [National University of Modern Languages (NUML)](https://numl.edu.pk), Islamabad, Pakistan, with an MS in Computer Science and a professional background in Science Education (M.Ed).

My research focuses on **Trustworthy AI and IoT Security**, particularly intrusion detection in smart home and smart healthcare networks using hybrid machine learning and deep learning models. My [published work](https://doi.org/10.3390/math10234598) proposes a KNN, Decision Tree and LSTM hybrid that addresses overfitting and generalization in IoT attack detection. I want to build on this toward AI systems that are reliable, secure and transparent.

Alongside research, I teach computer science and enjoy mentoring students, which shapes how I approach and communicate my work.

🎓 **I am seeking a fully funded PhD position at a European university.** If your group works on trustworthy AI, deep learning or IoT security, I would be glad to hear from you at [ana.shahid@numl.edu.pk](mailto:ana.shahid@numl.edu.pk).
